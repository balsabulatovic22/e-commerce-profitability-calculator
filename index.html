How it works Formulas & assumptions ＋

Use one product or an average order, over one period such as a month or campaign. Include shipping charged to customers in the selling price, and enter your full shipping cost separately.

Completed ordersShipped orders × (1 − failed / returned share)Revenue excluding VATCompleted orders × selling price ÷ (1 + VAT / 100)Operating profitRevenue excluding VAT − products − shipping − packaging − fees − ads − other fixed costsBreak-even ad budgetRevenue excluding VAT − all included costs other than adsBreak-even selling priceThe price at which revenue covers all entered costs, at the same order volume and return rate. Includes price-based payment fees and separated VAT.Break-even ROASCollected sales including VAT ÷ break-even ad budget

All entered orders have already shipped. Failed or returned orders generate no revenue, but still incur outbound shipping, packaging and any return shipping. Product loss determines how much returned stock is written off. Payment fees apply only to completed orders; add nonrefundable fees on returns to other fixed costs.

ROAS means return on ad spend. Here it uses collected sales after failed orders and refunds, so it may differ from your ad platform. Expected order counts may be fractional when you enter a return rate. Profit margin uses revenue excluding separated VAT.

Profit is before income or corporate tax and any costs you have not entered. Payment timing and inventory bought in advance are not modelled, so profit is not your cash balance. Break-even thresholds assume the same order volume. If the product loses money before advertising, there is no achievable break-even ROAS under these assumptions.
